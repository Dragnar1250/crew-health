Sources and limits of this prototype

  • Hazard model: NASA Human Research Program, the five hazards of human spaceflight (nasa.gov/hrp/risks, nasa.gov/hrp/radiation).
  • Reference ranges are general adult clinical ranges used as placeholders. A flight version would use each crew member's preflight baseline and NASA-STD-3001 limits.
  • Cerebrospinal fluid pressure is not measured directly. Optic nerve sheath diameter from eye ultrasound is used as an indirect sign, with a ground clinical threshold as a placeholder.
  • Environment dose rates are approximate published averages (ISS, lunar surface, Mars surface) and must be checked against the challenge resources.
  • Reports are simulated. Nothing is transmitted. This is not a medical device.
